What's actually happening

Fibromyalgia is a signaling problem, not a structural one.

This is why the standard work-up keeps coming back clean — and why a different kind of approach can help.

1

The volume gets stuck up

In fibromyalgia, the brain and spinal cord amplify pain signals — a process called central sensitization. Ordinary touch, temperature, or pressure gets read as pain.

2

The scans look fine

Because the issue lives in how signals are processed — not in a torn ligament or a damaged disc — standard imaging and bloodwork usually look normal.

3

It spreads beyond pain

The same overactive system drives the fatigue, the fog, the poor sleep, and the stress sensitivity. They're not separate problems — they're the same one.

4

A sensitized system can be re-trained

The nervous system is adaptable. With the right biological support and consistent retraining, an over-amplified system can learn to settle.

Questions people ask first.

Is this a cure for fibromyalgia?+

No. There is no cure for fibromyalgia, and any program claiming one should be treated with caution. The goal of the Reset is meaningful, lasting improvement in pain, energy, sleep, and function by helping an over-amplified nervous system settle. Results vary from person to person.

Do I need an official fibromyalgia diagnosis first?+

Not necessarily. Many people arrive without a formal diagnosis after years of being dismissed. Your evaluation with Dr. Lee includes a careful clinical assessment to understand what's driving your symptoms and whether this program is appropriate for you.

Is Low Dose Naltrexone (LDN) safe?+

LDN is generally well-tolerated and has an emerging body of research specifically in fibromyalgia. It's used off-label and is only prescribed after Dr. Lee reviews your history and confirms it's safe and appropriate for you. Not everyone is a candidate, and the program is not solely dependent on it.
